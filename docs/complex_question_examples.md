# Complex Question Examples

Use these to test that the assistant chooses the correct RT BDI location without
the user naming a report.

## Home dashboard

- How are we doing this month?
- Which stores are top 10 right now by PPD and accessory sales?
- What is the company conversion rate month-to-date?
- Compare today's snapshot with the month-to-date totals on Home.
- Which previous-day store is trending highest for MTD accessories?

## Employee + KPI + MRC

- Who is number one in phone sales, what is their plan mix, and how does their gross profit compare to #2?
- Top accessory seller: what store are they in and what is their KPI gross profit?
- Of the top 10 employees by activations, who has the lowest accessory attach?
- Who has the best gross profit per hour among employees with 30+ activations?
- Which employee sold the most insurance, and what was their conversion ratio?

## Sales / Finance / Trade-In

- Which employee financed the most dollars and what was their KPI gross profit?
- Total approved finance amount vs financed amount by store.
- Which finance company appears most, and which store uses it the most?
- How many trade-ins applied by carrier, make, and model?
- Which employee had the most trade-ins, and what is their conversion ratio?

## Inventory / Phone Trend

- How many Samsung phones are on hand and which store has the most?
- Which phones are fastest-selling but low on stock?
- Which items have high stock but low 30-day sales?
- What is inventory value for the top activation store?
- Compare the top store's phone inventory against its 30-day phone trend.

## Inventory operations

- Total transfer cost between stores last month, and which stores sent the most.
- Open PO amount by vendor and which stores have the most open amount.
- Which stores had the most unmatched audit items?
- Show tangible audit variance and who adjusted it.
- Find details for a serial number and whether it is aged over 30 days.

## Memory / follow-ups

- Who is #1 in phone sales?
- Which store?
- Their accessories?
- Their plan mix?
- Compare them to #2.
- What is their store's inventory value?
